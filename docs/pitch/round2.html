4

Risk engine v2 · all of Uttarakhand

Same formula; now every part is measured

score = rain × slope × history for every 10 km of road

R

Rain

Open-Meteo, last 24 h + next 6 h. All route options share one request, so we stay inside the free limit.

S

Slope 1.0 → 2.0

Our own slope map of the state from 110 AWS terrain tiles. 10° or less = 1.0, 35°+ = 2.0. Works offline, no API call. Haridwar 1°, Joshimath 25°, Gaurikund 33°.

H

History 1.0 → 1.8

100 placed landslides in Uttarakhand (NASA catalog 2007–2017) within 2 km of the road: +0.4 per slide per 10 km.

Example

Rishikesh → Kedarnath, safest route: 212 km, 5.3 h, steepest hills 29°, 23 past slides near the road. Under 2 mm/h of rain the 21 stretches score 9 to 25, not one flat number: the driver sees where the danger is.

5

Predict · three independent checks

Rain model, satellite and photos agree before we cry wolf

1 · AI landslide check

NASA LHASA 7-day rain index + a model trained on real slides on this road (2010–2014). Tested on 2015–2017: AUC 0.75; "High" caught 7 of 24 slide days, "Medium or High" 14 of 24.

2 · Satellite slide check

ESA Sentinel-2 (10 m, new pass ~5 days). Greenness (NDVI) and bare ground in 1 km, a month ago vs now. A slide strips plants, so a jump in bare ground flags it. No GPU, no paid service.

3 · AI photo check

Every report photo goes to Google Gemini (free tier): "road blocked / open / unclear" with a confidence. If the AI is down, the report still goes out.

Why three?

Rain says when, satellite says where the ground moved, photos say what drivers see now. Each one is shown with its source, so a judge or driver can check it.

Crowd reports you can trust

One prank must not close a highway

Two different phones

A road closes only when 2 phones report it within 1 km in 12 h. Each phone has a random id, nothing personal.

?

"Still there?"

Waze-style: drivers passing a report get one tap "Yes" or "No, road clear". "Clear" reopens the road for everyone.

AI

Photo check

The AI verdict is shown next to the report: "road looks blocked (90%)".

+

Rewards for truth

A report confirmed by another driver earns 3× the points, so true reports pay more than fake ones.

Reports work anywhere in the state. On a watched highway they attach to the stretch; elsewhere they show by place name ("Near Mussoorie") and still reach every phone instantly.

Usability · built for a taxi driver with one hand

What we tested on a real phone, and what we found

Design choices

  • Report in 2 taps while driving: 6 big round icons, sent from the car's position
  • Screen stays on during a trip, like Google Maps
  • Phone vibrates on every hazard alert, longer for a closed road
  • Voice: "go to <any place>", "start", "report", "SOS"
  • Every colour also in words ("High risk"); plain English; 48 px+ buttons
  • Offline: last map kept on the phone; SOS falls back to SMS

Blockers we hit, and how we handled them

Every blocker became a design rule

BlockerWhat we didRule we kept
Google News did not answer Vercel's build server; the whole deploy failedNews and AI check return "unavailable" and retry in minutes instead of breaking the pageNo outside service can take the app down
Open-Meteo rate limit (429) when we fetched slope for every routeBuilt our own slope map of the state (131 KB) and merged rain requestsStatic facts live in the app, not behind an API
NASA's live LHASA servers returned 404Re-implemented the published LHASA method and trained our own model on NASA slidesUse the science, not one server
App stuck on the splash logo on phonesOffline cache was breaking the video; skipped video chunks, added an 8 s exitA driver is never trapped on a screen
Google login ended in a blank browser tab in the Android appBridge page that hands the login back to the app by deep linkTest every flow on the phone, not only the browser
Paid AI API for photo checksMoved to Google Gemini free tier; report still sent if AI is offZero running cost for the prototype
Public router gives only one routeAsk again with a third of the road virtually closed to find the other valleysAlways show a real alternative
Prank or wrong reports2 different phones, "still there?", AI photo check, rewards for confirmed reportsTrust needs more than one voice
