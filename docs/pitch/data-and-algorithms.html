4 · Algorithms

Algorithm · codeHow it worksReference
Risk score
lib/risk.ts, lib/terrain.ts, lib/routes.ts
score = rain_score × slope_factor × history_factor (0–100)
rain_score = min(100, 1.5 × rain last 24 h + 4 × rain next 6 h) in mm (IMD calls 64.5 mm/day "heavy").
slope_factor = 1 + clamp((mean slope − 10°) / 25°, 0, 1) → 1.0 … 2.0.
history_factor = min(1.8, 1 + 0.4 × past slides within 2 km per 10 km of road).
Scored for every ~10 km of any route. 0–39 Low · 40–69 Medium · 70+ High.		Formula agreed by the team (AGENTS.md); slope and history from the data above
Safe time to leave
lib/risk.ts		The earliest hour in the next 12 hours when every stretch scores below 70.—
Indian rainfall thresholds
lib/thresholds.ts		For durations D = 1, 3, 6, 12, 24, 48, 72 h ending 6 h from now: mean intensity I = rain in D hours ÷ D. A stretch is "above threshold" if I ≥ a·Db for any D. NH-58: a = 58.7, b = −1.12. Chamoli–Joshimath: a = 1.82, b = −0.23.NHESS 2024; Landslides 2013 (links above)
AI landslide check
lib/ai-risk.ts, scripts/train-landslide-model.mjs		NASA LHASA antecedent rainfall index: ARI = Σ R(t−i)·(i+1)−2 / Σ (i+1)−2 over 7 days, divided by the stretch's own 95th percentile (2001–2020).
4 features: log(1 + ARI/ARI95), log(1 + rain today), log(1 + rain 14 days), monsoon month → logistic regression.
Trained on 2010–2014 slide days, tested on 2015–2017: AUC 0.75 (train 0.90); "High" = top 15% of days caught 7 of 24 test slide days, "Medium or High" (top 30%) caught 14 of 24. Re-scored every 30 min for today and tomorrow.		Kirschbaum & Stanley (2018), Earth's Future, doi.org/10.1002/2017EF000715; code github.com/nasa/lhasa
Slope map
scripts/build-slope-grid.mjs		For each terrain pixel: gradient = height difference to neighbours ÷ distance (east–west and north–south); slope = atan(√(gx² + gy²)). Averaged per 0.01° cell.Standard central-difference slope from neighbouring pixels
Satellite slide check
lib/satellite.ts		NDVI = (NIR − Red) / (NIR + Red) from Sentinel-2 bands B08 and B04 in the 1 km around a spot, newest clear image vs one ≥ 25 days older. "Possible new slide" when bare ground (NDVI < 0.2) rises by ≥ 10 points and mean NDVI falls by ≥ 0.1.Rouse et al. (1974) NDVI; method of HazMapper (Scheip & Wegmann, 2021, NHESS)
Routes and detours
lib/valhalla.ts, lib/routes.ts		Valhalla shortest path (time). Closed spots become ~1 km no-go boxes. Because the public server returns one route, we ask again with a point one-third and two-thirds along the road blocked, which finds the other valley roads; drop any option over 2× longer. "Safest" = lowest risk, then quickest.github.com/valhalla/valhalla
Road closed
lib/reports.ts		Closed when blocking reports (landslide, rockfall, road blocked) come from ≥ 2 different phones within 1 km in 12 h, and no "Road clear" report came after. Drivers passing within 500 m are asked "Still there?".—
Photo check
lib/photo-check.ts		Report photo sent to Google Gemini (gemini-2.5-flash) with a fixed prompt; answer forced into JSON {verdict: blocked / clear / unclear, confidence 0–1, reason}. Only photos from our own storage are accepted.ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs
Road Rewards
lib/rewards.ts		10 points per report; +20 when a different phone reports within 1 km and 12 h. Levels at 0, 50, 200 points.—

Limits: NASA catalog points are often placed to the nearest 1–10 km; satellite pixels are 10 m, so small rockfalls do not show; clouds hide the ground in monsoon. The score is a guide for drivers, not a guarantee.
