Himrakshak · Every dataset, where it comes from, and where it shows in the app

Indian state covered: Uttarakhand (all 13 districts). The AI check and Indian thresholds focus on the Rishikesh–Badrinath road (NH-7) through Tehri, Pauri, Rudraprayag and Chamoli. Team Elite Zero Division · Algonex 1.0 · 28 Nov 2026 · Live app: himrakshak.vercel.app · Code: github.com/Surajrana-git/himrakshak

All data is free and open. Links checked on 28 Nov 2026. Original copies saved in docs/data/original/ (NASA catalog CSV, 20-year rain CSV, 110 terrain tiles, Sentinel-2 images, Landslide Atlas PDF, NH-58 paper PDF); readable CSVs in docs/data/.

NASA Global Landslide Catalog (2007–2017)
https://data.nasa.gov/dataset/global-landslide-catalog-export		11,033 landslides worldwide; 1,265 in India; 189 in Uttarakhand. AWS Terrain Tiles (SRTM-based elevation)
