3 · Technical approach

Indian standards + live rain + driver ground truth

Risk = 100 × (0.40 × Sstatic + 0.60 × Tdynamic) (0–100, every 10 km)

Sstatic = TEHD / 10: BIS IS 14496 (Part 2) style hazard score from slope (whole-state elevation tiles) and past landslides within 2 km (NASA) · Tdynamic = 0.85 × max(rain past 24 h / 140 mm, rain next 6 h / 30 mm), Open-Meteo · a block confirmed by 2 drivers = 95 (red) · 0–39 Low, 40–69 Medium, 70+ High

Indian thresholds

NH-58 study I = 58.7·D−1.12 and Chamoli–Joshimath I = 1.82·D−0.23, checked over 1–72 h

AI landslide check

NASA LHASA 7-day rain index + logistic regression trained on 2010–14 slides; AUC 0.75 on 2015–17

Satellite slide check

Sentinel-2 NDVI now vs a month ago; bare ground +10 points flags a possible new slide

AI photo check

Gemini reads each report photo: road blocked or open, and rejects screenshots or gallery photos

App

Next.js + React + Tailwind on Vercel; Android app (Capacitor) with native SMS and Bluetooth

Data & live sync

Supabase: Postgres, realtime reports, photo storage, row-level security

Maps & routes

MapLibre + OpenStreetMap, Valhalla routing (car, bus, bike, walk), Esri satellite

Alerts

Firebase push for SOS and confirmed closures; voice + vibration in the app

Full dataset and algorithm list with links: Himrakshak-Data-and-Algorithms.pdf

6 · Survive without signal, and what comes next

When the slide comes down, one press reaches help three ways at once

① Internet

Pin on the rescue dashboard + push alert to every Himrakshak phone

+

② SMS

Sent by the app itself to family, with GPS; needs only a phone signal, no data; retries 3 times

+

③ Bluetooth mesh

No signal at all: phone to phone, ~50–100 m a hop, up to 6 hops; the first phone with internet uploads it once

Compact packet, one SMS or one Bluetooth advert: HIM|k3j9a2|30.35800,77.84940|STUCK|1795849329|6 · the same id on every copy, so the dashboard shows one pin
