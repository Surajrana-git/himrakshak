A Google-Maps-style navigation app for Char Dham pilgrim roads that knows about landslides. It shows the safest and fastest road, warns the driver while he drives, reroutes around closures and keeps SOS one tap away, even with no signal.

For Gautam (design): every screen here is a working layout taken from the code and the redesign spec. Please redraw, improve and send back your version. The last page lists exactly what to return. Design language

“Milestone and Chevron”: everything comes from the Garhwal road, not from an app template

Every colour also says its word: कम खतरा Low · मध्यम खतरा Medium · ज़्यादा खतरा High · बंद Closed · जाम Jam · धीमा Slow · साफ़ Clear · जानकारी नहीं No data.

Type

OSWALD — HEADINGS, KM, CLOCKS, COUNTDOWN
Public Sans for body text. Devanagari uses the phone's system font (Noto Sans Devanagari in this PDF). Each driver string has two lines: Hindi big on top, English small below.

Never

Purple or blue gradients, glass or blur, emoji as icons, “Welcome to…”, hero text, stock or AI art, lorem ipsum, colour without a word.

Signature elements

NH 7
श्रीनगर
SRINAGAR
23 km

1. Milestone (km stone). An orange cap with the NH number and a snow body with the town (Hindi big, English small) and km. Used for the next town in navigation, route card heads and the ahead list.

बंद · CLOSED

2. Hazard plate. A road-sign triangle with our own line icon (landslide, rockfall, blocked, water, jam, clear). A closed road is an ink plate “बंद · CLOSED”. The same plate is used on pins, alerts and the legend.

3. BRO chevrons. Ink and orange stripes, used in only two places: the countdown strip in alerts, and the border of every DEMO, REPLAY or SIMULATION control, so judges see honesty at a glance.

Ergonomics (taxi driver, one thumb)

  • Tap targets are at least 48 px; primary actions are 64 px. Everything important sits in the bottom 60% of the screen.
  • Right rail: SOS (56, danger) above Report (64, orange). These are always there, also while navigating.
  • Two modes: planning is a snow sheet over a light map; driving is an ink HUD (milestone on top, bar at the bottom).
  • Mobile first at 375 px. On desktop the sheet becomes a 400 px left panel.
  • Motion is CSS only (150–250 ms). The countdown strip drains left to right.
